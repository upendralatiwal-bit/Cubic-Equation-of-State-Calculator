Theory

Generic cubic EOS: P = RT/(V−b) − a(T)/[(V+εb)(V+σb)], with a(T) = Ψ α(Tr) R²Tc²/Pc and b = Ω R Tc/Pc.

In terms of Z, with A = aP/(RT)² and B = bP/RT, it becomes a cubic solved analytically (Cardano): Z³ + [(ε+σ)B − 1 − B]Z² + [A + εσB² − (ε+σ)B(1+B)]Z − [AB + εσB²(1+B)] = 0. The largest root is the vapour, the smallest the liquid; when three roots exist the stable phase is the one with the lowest Gibbs energy (lowest φ).

Residual properties (q = A/B, I = ln[(Z+σB)/(Z+εB)]/(σ−ε); for vdW I = B/Z):

HR/RT = Z − 1 + [d ln α/d ln Tr − 1] q I
SR/R = ln(Z − B) + (d ln α/d ln Tr) q I

Total properties relative to ideal gas at (T₀, P₀), with Cpig/R = A + BT + CT² + DT⁻²:
H = ∫CpigdT + HR,   S = ∫CpigdT/T − R ln(P/P₀) + SR

Pure-component fugacity: ln φ = Z − 1 − ln(Z − B) − q I,   f = φP.
